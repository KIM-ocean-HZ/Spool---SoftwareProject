Why it exists

An AI forgets your project the moment you close the tab, so you explain it again. And again. Meanwhile the details sit scattered across chats, emails and tabs you closed weeks ago. Spool keeps them in one place, so one paste brings any AI up to speed.

How it works

Save it, keep it, paste it

01 · SAVE

One key. Nothing to decide.

Copy anything, then tap ⌥ twice. It goes straight into the project you are working on, along with where it came from — down to the browser tab. A small note appears in the corner to confirm. The main window never opens, so you never lose your place or your train of thought.

The real thing: the lines you selected on the page, and Spool's note floating over the corner — what got saved, where it went, and undo.

02 · KEEP

A list, not a chat.

Everything you save stacks up in time order under one project. Two levels only — workspace, then project. No folders to build, no tags to pick, nothing to tidy up. Come back next week and the newest notes show you exactly where you stopped.

Jul 14 · acme.com · Safari

Acme is hiring a data analyst: SQL required, Python a plus.

Jul 29 · you

Resume summary first, then the cover letter.

03 · PASTE

One click, ready to paste.

Spool writes the project out as plain text: your own words first, things you read kept as sources, anything an AI wrote marked so you can check it. Paste it into any AI — or just read it yourself to get back into the work.

The real Pack window: five notes become 3,899 characters of ready-to-paste text. Two choices, one button.

What makes it different

Three promises

Saving costs one key

If saving takes more than one key, people stop doing it. Every other decision in Spool follows from that one — no window to switch to, no box to fill in, no choice to make.

Nothing loses its source

Every note keeps its time and where it came from. Months later you can still tell what you decided yourself from what you simply read somewhere — and so can any AI you show it to.

AI helps, and signs its name

Connect the AI apps you already pay for. They can read, search and add notes, and every addition is signed. What you wrote by hand, no machine is allowed to change.

The app

Quiet on purpose

Warm paper, one accent colour, no badges fighting for your attention. Spool stays out of the way until you have something to save.

One project, five notes: your goal, a job posting, an answer from an AI chat, your own decision, an email from the recruiter. Each one keeps its time and its source.

Packing a project: pick how much to include, copy, paste. Five notes become 3,899 characters of ready-to-paste text.

MCP — beyond pasting

Let your AI read your notes directly

A pack is one paste. With the Model Context Protocol, the AI apps you already use can open your notes whenever they need them: read weeks of context, search every project, and put what they find back in the right place. Ten tools, two switches you turn on yourself, and no server anywhere.

One plain question, and the AI reads your workspaces, projects and note counts straight off your Mac.

One paragraph built from notes you saved over three weeks, from four different places. This is the part a single paste cannot do.

Every result keeps its source: what you read somewhere, what an AI suggested, and what you decided yourself.

Ask it to file a conclusion and it writes one note — with a short reason, and a link to the note it builds on.

  • Notes only leave when your own AI app reads them, and only after you switch that on
