How it works

Save it, keep it, paste it

01 · SAVE

One key. Nothing to decide.

Copy anything, then tap ⌥ twice. It goes straight into the project you are working on, along with where it came from — down to the browser tab. The confirmation pops up with the cursor already in a note box: type the thought that made you save this and press Enter — or click anywhere else to skip. Your own words outlast the excerpt, and they are what an AI reads first.

The real thing: the lines you selected on the page, and Spool's note floating over the corner — what got saved, where it went, and a box for the thought you had.

02 · KEEP

A list, not a chat.

Everything you save stacks up in time order under one project. Two levels only — workspace, then project. No folders to build, no tags to pick, nothing to tidy up. Come back next week and the newest notes show you exactly where you stopped.

Jul 14 · acme.com · Safari

Acme is hiring a data analyst: SQL required, Python a plus.

Jul 29 · you

Resume summary first, then the cover letter.

03 · PASTE

One click, ready to paste.

Spool writes the project out as plain text: your own words first, things you read kept as sources, anything an AI wrote marked so you can check it. Paste it into any AI — or just read it yourself to get back into the work.

The real Pack window: five notes become 3,899 characters of ready-to-paste text. Two choices, one button.

What makes it different

Three promises

Saving costs one key

If saving takes more than one key, people stop doing it. Every other decision in Spool follows from that one — no window to switch to, no box to fill in, no choice to make.

Nothing loses its source

Every note keeps its time and where it came from. Months later you can still tell what you decided yourself from what you simply read somewhere — and so can any AI you show it to.

AI helps, and signs its name

Connect the AI apps you already pay for. They can read, search and add notes, and every addition is signed. What you wrote by hand, no machine is allowed to change.
